# SEEDS Sustainability Decision Record (SEEDS-DR) - Example

> **Context:** Example generated for a patient-facing digital health system (Patient Portal)[cite: 7].  
> **Framework:** SEEDS (Sustainable Requirements Engineering in Digital Health Software)  
> **Repository:** [seeds-framework](https://github.com/julianash/seeds-framework)

---

## Record Identification & Context

* **SEEDS-DR ID:** SEEDS-DR-001[cite: 7]
* **Decision Context:** Existing digital health system assessment[cite: 7]
* **System or Solution:** Patient portal[cite: 7]
* **Stakeholder Role:** Patient/common user[cite: 7]

---

## Sustainability Reasoning

* **Sustainability Concern:**  
  Essential health-service information may be difficult to understand for users with different levels of digital literacy[cite: 7].

* **SEEDS Dimension(s):**  
  Individual; Social[cite: 7]

* **Related SEEDS Criterion:**  
  Usability; Accessibility; Transparency[cite: 7]

* **Expected Sustainability Effect:**  
  First-order and second-order individual/social effects ensuring equitable access to health information and reducing cognitive burden for vulnerable populations.

---

## Actionable Engineering & Verification

* **Derived Non-Functional Requirement (NFR) / Decision:**  
  The system shall present essential health-service information in plain language and accessible formats[cite: 7].

* **Acceptance Criterion:**  
  Representative users shall be able to complete key information-seeking tasks without assistance; critical information shall be reviewed for plain-language adequacy; the interface shall conform to applicable accessibility criteria[cite: 7].

* **Verification Evidence:**  
  Usability test report; accessibility audit; readability review; user feedback[cite: 7].

---

## Governance & Trade-offs

* **Trade-offs & Dependencies:**  
  Plain language must preserve clinical accuracy, legal clarity, and institutional responsibility[cite: 7].

* **Decision Status:**  
  Requires human review[cite: 7]

* **Human Review Notes:**  
  Review with patient representatives, healthcare professionals, and institutional communication specialists[cite: 7].
